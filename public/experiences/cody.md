# What is CODY Web Development?

CODY Web Development specializes in delivering cutting-edge solutions for employee monitoring, team collaboration, and productivity optimization. By leveraging advanced frontend technologies, the company develops robust tools to meet diverse client needs, particularly in the Japanese market.

## Professional Overview

At CODY Web Development, I contributed to two major projects, **ARISE** and **BizMake**, for Japanese-based clients. As both a Lead Frontend Engineer and a Frontend Engineer, I delivered impactful features and improvements. Collaborating with international teams, I focused on optimizing performance, enhancing usability, and delivering high-quality solutions on tight deadlines.

**September 2020 – December 2020 (4 months)**

As the Lead Frontend Engineer for the **ARISE employee monitoring platform**, I developed a comprehensive system that tracked and visualized key employee metrics, including:

#### Key Contributions:
