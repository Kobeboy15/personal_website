# SheepCRM: Revolutionizing Membership Management

SheepCRM is the UK's leading membership management software, designed to save time, automate administrative tasks, and empower organizations to grow their memberships. With a user-focused, intuitive, and high-performance platform, SheepCRM simplifies operations while fostering community engagement and satisfaction.

---

## Professional Overview

Collaborated with SheepCRM to manage, maintain, and enhance their industry-leading membership management platform. Played a pivotal role in the development of key features, including:

- **SheepCRM Calendar Events:**
  A comprehensive event management tool enabling seamless planning and scheduling through an intuitive visual interface.

- **SheepCRM Help Launcher Guide:**
  A context-sensitive guidance system providing real-time support, Intercom integration, and micro-service monitoring, significantly enhancing user experience.

Beyond feature development, responsibilities included managing Intercom tickets, implementing client-facing hot fixes, and streamlining operational workflows. Leveraging modern tools and frameworks, I contributed to SheepCRM’s mission of simplifying administrative tasks and supporting organizational growth.
